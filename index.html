Biography

I am a PhD researcher (2024–2027) at The University of Queensland, working with CSIRO Robotics to automate the design of robust soft robotic grippers using physics-based simulation, topology optimisation and machine learning.

Previously, I completed a Bachelor of Advanced Science with First Class Honours in Physics (2019–2023) at The University of Queensland, where I carried out experimental research on superconducting qubits with Dr. Arkady Fedorov. For my honours thesis, I developed a machine learning framework to approximate Feynman path integrals.
