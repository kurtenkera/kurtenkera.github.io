Biography

I am a PhD candidate at The University of Queensland and CSIRO Robotics in Brisbane, Australia, advised by David Howard, Josh Pinskier and Marcus Gallagher. My research focuses on the computational design of soft robotic grippers: combining topology optimization, dynamic finite-element simulation, and machine learning to automatically generate bespoke, manufacturable grippers, which I then fabricate and validate on physical hardware.

I am also a Machine Learning Engineer at Waymo, where I develop world models to improve the simulation of long-tail driving scenarios. Before my PhD, I completed a Bachelor of Science with Honours in Physics, where my honours thesis applied normalizing flows to Feynman path integrals.

I am broadly interested in AI for science and engineering design — in particular, using simulation and learning to solve physical design problems that are hard to specify by hand.
